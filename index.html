03 — About

Calm process, sharp details.

I'm Amit Halder, a Designer & Developer based in New Delhi, India. I care about the details people feel but rarely notice: spacing, timing, and the small moments between clicks.

I start with the people and the problem, long before I open a design tool. Good questions early save weeks of rework later.

A rough, clickable idea beats a long document. I put real ones in front of real users fast, then refine what they actually touch.

Launch is a checkpoint, not a finish line. I measure, listen, and keep improving, with clean handoff so the work is easy to own.
